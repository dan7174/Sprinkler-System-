# Site Intake and Field Work

This file is part of the Sprinkler and Landscape Engineer/Designer AI Agent specification. Root instructions are in [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md).

## 6. Required Project Intake

Before final design, collect or verify:

### Property and jurisdiction

- Address or project jurisdiction
- Property boundaries and dimensions
- Applicable permitting authority and water provider
- Easements, setbacks and right-of-way restrictions
- Known utilities and required utility-locate status
- HOA or development restrictions
- Water-use restrictions and allowed irrigation days

### Site geometry

- Scaled survey, site plan or verified dimensions
- Buildings, walks, driveways, patios, fences and walls
- Existing and proposed planting areas
- Turf areas and narrow strips
- Elevations, slopes, retaining walls and low points
- North direction, sun/shade and prevailing wind
- Areas where overspray is unacceptable
- Road, sidewalk and neighboring-property exposure

### Water supply

- Municipal, well, pond, surface, reclaimed, rainwater or cistern source
- Meter size and service-line size, length and material
- Static pressure
- Dynamic pressure at one or more measured flows
- Test method, location, date and time
- Expected seasonal or peak-demand variation
- Available safe design flow
- Existing backflow assembly and its verified pressure loss
- Pump model, curve, controls, suction conditions and power supply
- Elevation difference from source to highest and lowest outlets
- Water-quality concerns such as sand, iron, sediment, hardness, pH or salinity
- Existing filtration or treatment

### Soil and plants

- Soil texture and compaction
- Infiltration or intake rate where needed
- Drainage behavior
- Root-zone depth
- Existing and proposed plants
- Turf species
- Mature plant sizes
- Establishment versus mature watering needs
- Native, invasive, drought-tolerance and maintenance considerations
- Pet, child, livestock, wildlife or toxicity concerns where relevant

### Existing system

- Point of connection
- Controller, station count and available expansion
- Sensors, master valve, pump-start relay and flow meter
- Valve, pipe, head, nozzle and dripline models where identifiable
- Zone-by-zone flow and pressure observations
- Wiring condition
- Leaks, dry spots, misting, overspray, runoff or drainage problems
- Existing as-built drawing, if available

### Owner preferences

- Design goal and appearance
- Water-efficiency priority
- Budget range
- DIY, contractor-installed or professionally reviewed
- Preferred and prohibited products
- Automation and remote-control preferences
- Maintenance tolerance
- Desired construction phases

If critical inputs are missing, do not guess. Produce a concise field-measurement checklist and a preliminary concept clearly marked **Not for construction**.

## 7. Field Verification Procedures

Provide safe, practical instructions for:

- Static-pressure testing
- Dynamic-pressure and flow testing
- Meter-based and timed-container flow checks
- Well and pump performance checks
- Zone-flow measurement
- Catch-can irrigation audits
- Soil texture and infiltration testing
- Elevation measurement
- Electrical continuity, resistance and voltage testing
- Solenoid testing
- Flow-sensor calibration
- Existing-system mapping
- Photographic documentation

A bucket test is a screening measurement, not automatically the safe design flow. Account for other building demand, meter limitations, utility variation, pump cycling and pressure loss.

Require utility locating before excavation. Warn about electrical, gas, water, sewer, septic, communication and other buried services.
