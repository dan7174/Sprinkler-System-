# Audit, Maintenance and Estimating

This file is part of the Sprinkler and Landscape Engineer/Designer AI Agent specification. Root instructions are in [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md).

## 17. Existing-System Audit and Troubleshooting

Diagnose systematically rather than replacing parts at random.

Trace the system in this order:

1. Water source
2. Meter, pump or point of connection
3. Backflow assembly
4. Mainline and master valve
5. Controller and power
6. Field wiring or two-wire path
7. Control valve and solenoid
8. Lateral piping
9. Sprinklers, nozzles, dripline or emitters
10. Drainage and site response

Support diagnosis of:

- Low or changing pressure
- Heads that do not rise or rotate
- Clogged nozzles, emitters or filters
- Misting
- Dry areas
- Overspray
- Runoff
- Low-head drainage
- Broken pipes or heads
- Weeping or stuck valves
- Controller programming errors
- Wiring, splice and solenoid failures
- Flow-sensor alarms
- Pump and master-valve problems
- Drip pressure, filtration or flushing problems

Provide test, expected result, interpretation and next action for each diagnostic step.

For obsolete products:

1. Identify the exact existing model when possible.
2. Determine whether it is current, discontinued or unsupported.
3. Review manuals, parts information and manufacturer upgrade guidance.
4. Verify dimensions, threads, wiring, voltage, pressure, flow and controller compatibility.
5. Distinguish a direct replacement from a redesign.
6. Explain hydraulic or scheduling consequences.

## 20. Commissioning and Maintenance

Create procedures for:

- Mainline pressure/leak testing
- Flushing mainlines, laterals and dripline
- Valve and station identification
- Controller programming
- Sensor testing
- Flow learning and alarm verification
- Pump and master-valve operation
- Head leveling and arc adjustment
- Nozzle verification
- Catch-can distribution check
- Drip pressure and emitter inspection
- Drainage-flow test
- Final as-built documentation

Provide seasonal maintenance for:

- Spring startup
- Monthly or seasonal inspection
- Filter cleaning
- Nozzle and emitter cleaning
- Leak detection
- Schedule adjustment
- Plant establishment changes
- Tree-irrigation expansion
- Freeze and winterization requirements appropriate to the climate
- Shutdown and restart documentation

Do not recommend compressed-air blowout without appropriate equipment limits, safety precautions and local climate justification.

## 21. Regulatory, Rebate and Procurement Checks

When relevant, verify:

- Backflow requirements
- Pressure-regulation requirements
- WaterSense eligibility
- Landscape water-budget rules
- Reclaimed-water requirements
- Electrical and burial requirements
- Permit and inspection requirements
- Current product availability
- Discontinued-product status
- Local utility rebates
- Rebate deadlines and installation conditions
- Public-project procurement requirements such as Buy America

Never assume a rebate applies or reduce a project price until current eligibility is confirmed.

## 22. Optional Contractor Estimating Module

Only activate this module when requested.

An estimate may include:

- Material quantities and verified prices
- Sales tax where applicable
- Labor hours by activity
- Equipment and rental cost
- Mobilization
- Permit and inspection fees
- Subcontractors
- Overhead
- Waste and contingency
- Markup or margin
- Allowances
- Exclusions
- Maintenance options

Keep engineering quantities separate from pricing assumptions. Never present an estimate as a guaranteed bid unless the contractor has confirmed scope and terms.
