# Field Measurement Checklist

For collecting the inputs a final irrigation design requires
(docs/03-site-intake-and-field-work.md). Fill in every item or mark it
N/A with a reason. Until the critical items are verified, any design is
preliminary and **Not for construction**.

Safety first: call 811 (utility locate) before any digging. Do not open
electrical panels or work on line-voltage wiring — that requires a
licensed electrician.

## 1. Water supply (critical)

- [ ] Water source type (municipal / well / other): ____________
- [ ] Meter size (on the meter body or the water bill): ______ in
- [ ] Service line size / material / approx. length: ______ in, ____________, ______ ft
- [ ] **Static pressure**: gauge on an outside faucet, all water OFF.
      Reading: ______ psi  Date/time: ____________
- [ ] **Dynamic pressure at measured flow** (required — static alone is
      not enough): with the gauge on one faucet, open a second faucet
      and time how long it takes to fill a 5-gallon bucket.
      Fill time: ______ s → flow = 300 / seconds = ______ gpm
      Gauge reading while flowing: ______ psi  Date/time: ____________
- [ ] Was any other water running in the house during the test? Y / N
- [ ] Existing backflow device (type/model if labeled): ____________
- [ ] Elevation change, water source to highest point watered: ______ ft
      (uphill or downhill?)

## 2. Site geometry (critical)

- [ ] Property sketch with measurements of each lawn/bed area
      (tape measure or measuring wheel; note north arrow)
- [ ] Locations of buildings, driveway, walks, patio, fences
- [ ] Areas where overspray is unacceptable (siding, windows, sidewalk,
      neighbor): ____________
- [ ] Narrow strips (width ______ ft) and odd corners
- [ ] Slopes: where, and roughly how steep (rise over run): ____________
- [ ] Sun/shade pattern notes: ____________

## 3. Soil and plants

- [ ] Soil texture (squeeze a moist handful: gritty = sandy,
      smooth/sticky = clay, in between = loam): ____________
- [ ] How fast does a puddle soak in? Fast / medium / slow
- [ ] Turf areas vs. shrub/tree/bed areas marked on the sketch
- [ ] Trees that need dedicated watering: ____________

## 4. Existing equipment (if any)

- [ ] Controller make/model and station count: ____________
- [ ] Valve locations and any model markings: ____________
- [ ] Head/nozzle models visible on caps: ____________
- [ ] Known problems (dry spots, leaks, misting, runoff): ____________

## 5. Rules and preferences

- [ ] Water provider watering restrictions/schedule: ____________
- [ ] Backflow requirements per water provider (call or website): ____________
- [ ] Budget range and DIY vs. contractor: ____________
- [ ] Smart-controller / app-control preference: ____________

## Photo list

Take photos of: the meter, the gauge readings (static and flowing), the
main shutoff, any existing backflow device, the controller and its
wiring label, one photo of each yard area from a corner.
